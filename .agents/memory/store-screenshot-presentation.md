---
name: Store screenshot presentation
description: User's required interface-only treatment for store screenshots
---

Store screenshots should be cut at the top to show only the app interface, not
the battery percentage, signal, or other phone status indicators.

**Why:** The user corrected screenshots that included those indicators.

**How to apply:** Remove the OS status bar from store screenshot deliverables.
Preserve original captures and ensure final exports meet store dimensions without
cutting off app content. Do not present older mockups as current release captures.
