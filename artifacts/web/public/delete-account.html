Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your 333 Lives account and associated account records at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

1
In the app: Go to your Profile → tap "Delete account", type DELETE, and confirm. The app removes your account records and local app data, then signs you out.
2
If the app request fails: Contact support@333lives.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. Support can verify and help complete the request.
3
What happens: Account records and local app data are removed. Associated media cleanup is best effort. Authentication deletion is requested through Clerk and retried when appropriate; some media or operational copies may take additional time to clear.
4
After submitting: The app signs you out after a successful request. If Clerk deletion still fails after a retry, use the support fallback above.
