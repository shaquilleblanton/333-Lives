Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your 333 Lives account and all associated data at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

1
In the app: Go to your Profile → tap Settings → tap "Delete Account" to delete directly from the app.
2
By email: Send a deletion request to 333livesenterprises@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account.
3
What gets deleted: Your profile, all journal entries, intentions, habits, life events, voice memos, uploaded files, and any other personal data associated with your account.
4
Timeline: Account and data deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.
